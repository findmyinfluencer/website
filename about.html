Built to make discovery effortless

Find My Influencer started with a simple frustration: brands and creators were spending more time searching for each other than actually creating. So we built a platform that does the searching for you.

Our story

Find My Influencer launched in 2026 out of a simple observation: Indian brands were spending weeks manually scouting creators on Instagram and YouTube, while genuinely talented influencers, especially outside the big metros, were going undiscovered.

We built a platform that puts verified, searchable creator data front and centre, so a brand can go from "we need a beauty creator in Bengaluru" to a shortlist of real, engaged profiles in minutes, not weeks.

Today, thousands of creators and hundreds of brands run campaigns through the platform every month, connecting faster than ever with clear budgets and deliverables agreed upfront.
