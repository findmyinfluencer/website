10 Tips for Running a Successful Influencer Campaign in India

FMI Find My Influencer · July 24, 2026 · 6 min read

Running your first influencer campaign can feel overwhelming: hundreds of creators, dozens of platforms, and no clear way to tell who's actually worth the budget. After helping brands launch thousands of campaigns on Find My Influencer, we've distilled what separates campaigns that convert from campaigns that just generate likes.

1. Start with a single, measurable goal

“Brand awareness” isn't a goal, it's a wish. Before you shortlist a single creator, decide whether you're optimising for reach, engagement, traffic or direct sales. Every other decision in this list flows from that one choice.

2. Pick niche fit over follower count

A beauty creator with 40K highly-engaged followers in your target city will almost always outperform a lifestyle generalist with 400K scattered, disengaged ones. Use niche and engagement rate as your primary filters, not raw follower count.

3. Brief creators, don't script them

The campaigns that feel authentic, and therefore perform, give creators a clear brief (key message, must-include details, deadline) and then get out of the way. Over-scripted content reads as an ad, and audiences scroll past ads.

  • Share 2-3 key talking points, not a word-for-word script
  • Give creators creative freedom on format (Reels vs Stories vs static)
  • Always include your campaign hashtag and tagging requirements upfront
"The best-performing campaigns we've tracked all shared one thing: brands trusted the creator's voice instead of trying to replace it with their own."

4. Mix creator sizes deliberately

A single celebrity post can spike awareness overnight, but a layer of 10-20 micro-influencers sustains conversation for weeks afterward. Most successful campaigns on the platform combine both tiers rather than betting everything on one big name.

5. Agree on payment terms in writing, upfront

Payment disputes are the single biggest reason creator relationships sour. Before work begins, confirm the amount, timeline and payment method directly with the creator in writing, so there's no ambiguity about what's owed and when.

6. Track engagement rate, not just views

A Reel with 50K views and 200 comments is doing more for your brand than one with 200K views and 40 comments. Ask creators for their engagement rate on recent posts before locking in a partnership.

7. Give campaigns at least 3-4 weeks

Rushed campaigns produce rushed content. Creators need time to genuinely try a product before they can talk about it convincingly, and audiences can tell the difference.

8. Repurpose creator content in paid media

The highest-performing paid ads we've seen on the platform aren't brand-produced, they're creator content, boosted. Always negotiate usage rights upfront so you can repurpose top-performing organic posts into paid campaigns.

9. Review applicants, don't just accept the first ones

Open campaigns on Find My Influencer typically get 10-20 applicants within 48 hours. Take the time to compare profiles side by side rather than accepting whoever applies first: niche fit matters more than speed.

10. Measure, then run it again

Every campaign is a data point for the next one. Track which creators, formats and niches actually moved your goal metric, and double down on what worked for your next campaign.
