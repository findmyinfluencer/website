Building a Content Series Brands Want to Sponsor

FMI Find My Influencer · September 20, 2026 · 5 min read

A series gives brands a predictable, recurring placement

A weekly or monthly series offers a brand a known, repeatable slot to sponsor, which is an easier yes than a one-off post with no established format.

Pick a format your audience already watches weekly

The strongest series formats are ones an audience already engages with consistently — adding a sponsor to an existing habit works better than inventing a new one for the brand.

Build the series before you pitch it, not after

A series with two or three episodes already live is a far easier pitch than a concept alone. Brands sponsor what they can already see working.

A series makes renewing a deal the default, not a negotiation

Once a brand is part of an ongoing series, renewing becomes the natural next step rather than a fresh negotiation each time.

Pitch the series, not just a single collaboration

When reaching out to brands, lead with the series format and its existing audience response, not a single post idea.
