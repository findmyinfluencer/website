How to Choose the Right Niche Influencers for a Product Launch

FMI Find My Influencer · April 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Product launches carry more pressure than always-on campaigns: there's usually one window to make an impression, and a wrong-fit creator roster can quietly undermine an otherwise strong product. The good news is that shortlisting for a launch is a filtering problem, not a guessing game, if you approach it in the right order.

Filter 1: Niche relevance, not adjacent interest

A fitness creator posting about a new protein bar is niche-relevant. A general lifestyle creator posting about the same bar because they “like snacks” is adjacent at best. For launches specifically, favour tight niche relevance over broader reach, since the audience needs to immediately understand why this creator is credible talking about this product.

Filter 2: Audience location and demographic match

If your launch is city-specific or targets a particular age group, filter for creators whose audience breakdown actually matches, not just creators based in that city. A creator living in Mumbai can still have a majority-Delhi audience; check the actual audience data, not the creator's own location.

Filter 3: Content style fit

Look at a creator's last 10-15 posts and ask honestly whether your product would look native in their feed or obviously bolted on. A launch benefits from content that doesn't read as an ad, and that starts with picking creators whose existing style the product genuinely suits.

Filter 4: Engagement quality over volume

For a launch specifically, prioritise creators with a track record of comments that read as genuine questions or reactions, not just emoji strings. Product launches often succeed or fail on whether the audience asks questions and generates conversation, which is a stronger leading indicator than view count alone.

Putting it together

Run your shortlist through all four filters in order, niche, location and demographics, style fit, then engagement quality, rather than starting from follower count and working backward.
