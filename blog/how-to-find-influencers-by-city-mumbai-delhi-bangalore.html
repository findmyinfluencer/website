How to Find Influencers by City: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore and Beyond

FMI Find My Influencer · September 6, 2026 · 6 min read

Brands running a store opening, a dealership event or a city-specific launch often search for influencers the same way a national brand would, by niche first. For local campaigns, city should be the first filter, not an afterthought.

Audience location, not creator location

A creator who lives in Bangalore but has an audience spread nationally isn't a local creator for search purposes. Filter by where a creator's audience actually lives, not their own city of residence.

Metro cities need niche filtering too

Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore each have thousands of creators across every niche. A city filter alone still returns too broad a list, combine it with niche and follower tier to get a workable shortlist.

Tier-2 and tier-3 cities have thinner but higher-trust creator pools

Cities like Indore, Coimbatore or Lucknow have fewer creators overall, but the ones who exist often carry outsized local trust precisely because of that scarcity.

Multi-city campaigns need a city-by-city shortlist

Rather than one national shortlist, build a separate shortlist per city for multi-location launches, since a creator strong in one city's audience may have almost no following in another.

Search by city and niche together
