How to Find Instagram Influencers in India: A Brand's Complete Guide

FMI Find My Influencer · September 4, 2026 · 6 min read

Searching "Instagram influencers in India" returns endless directories and generic lists that rarely match what a specific brand actually needs. The real work isn't finding influencers who exist, it's filtering down to the ones who fit your product, audience and budget.

Start with niche and audience location together

Niche alone isn't enough. A beauty creator with a Mumbai-heavy audience and one with a mostly tier-2 audience serve very different campaigns, even if both post identical content. Filter by niche and audience location together, not one after the other.

Follower tiers matter less than fit

Nano (1K-10K), micro (10K-100K) and macro (100K+) Instagram creators each play a different role, awareness versus conversion versus trust. Decide which role you need filled before sorting by follower count.

Check Reels performance, not just feed posts

An account with strong feed engagement but flat Reels views is a different bet than one performing well across formats, since Reels currently drive the bulk of new-audience discovery on the platform.

Use a platform built for filtering, not a hashtag search

Manually scrolling hashtags surfaces the same popular accounts everyone else already books. Find My Influencer's influencer search lets you filter directly by niche, location, engagement rate and follower tier in one pass.

Shortlist before you reach out

Build a shortlist of 8-10 accounts before messaging anyone. It gives you a comparison baseline and avoids anchoring your whole campaign to the first profile that looked promising.

Once you've built a shortlist, knowing what separates real engagement from inflated numbers matters. See our guide on spotting fake followers and engagement.
