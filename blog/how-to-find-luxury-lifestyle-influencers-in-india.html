How to Find Luxury and Lifestyle Influencers in India

FMI Find My Influencer · October 10, 2026 · 5 min read

Engagement rate matters more than reach at the luxury tier

A luxury audience is smaller by definition. Filtering by engagement rate rather than raw followers surfaces creators whose audience actually has purchasing intent.

Aesthetic consistency is the real filter, not category tags

Two creators tagged “lifestyle” can look nothing alike. Reviewing a creator's actual grid for visual and tonal consistency with the brand matters more than the category label.

Verified and top-rated badges help shortlist faster

Status badges on a creator's profile are a useful first filter when narrowing a premium shortlist, before a closer manual review of each account.

Smaller luxury-adjacent creators often outperform mega accounts

A mid-size creator with a genuinely affluent, engaged audience regularly outperforms a mega-influencer whose audience skews broader than the brand's actual buyer.

Build a shortlist, don't book off the first search page

Luxury campaigns are harder to undo publicly than mass-market ones. Comparing several creators' past brand work before booking is worth the extra day it takes.
