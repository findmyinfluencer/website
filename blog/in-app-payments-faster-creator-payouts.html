New: In-App Payments for Faster Creator Payouts

FMI Find My Influencer · August 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Getting paid after a campaign has historically meant creators chasing invoices over email or DM, with brands managing payments outside the platform entirely. In-App Payments closes that gap, letting brands pay creators directly once deliverables are approved.

What's new

Once a brand marks a campaign deliverable as approved, the creator receives a payout request inside their dashboard with the agreed amount pre-filled from the campaign brief, ready to release with one click.

  • Payouts tied directly to approved deliverables, no manual invoicing
  • Full payment history visible to both brand and creator
  • Split payments supported for multi-part deliverables

Why we built this

Late or missed payments were consistently the top complaint we heard from creators, often caused by payment simply falling through the cracks of an email thread rather than any dispute over the work. Bringing payment into the same flow as approval removes that gap.

Where to find it

Log in, open any campaign with an approved deliverable, and select "Release Payment." Creators can track payout status from their own dashboard at any time.
