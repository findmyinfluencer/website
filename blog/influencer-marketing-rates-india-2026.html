Influencer Marketing Rates in India 2026: What Brands Should Expect to Pay

FMI Find My Influencer · August 10, 2026 · 6 min read

"What should we budget?" is one of the most common questions brands ask before their first campaign, and it's a harder one to answer than most industries because rates in influencer marketing are still far less standardised than traditional ad pricing.

Rates by follower tier

Nano creators (1K-10K) typically charge for a single Instagram post, often in exchange for product plus a modest fee. Micro creators (10K-100K) usually price per deliverable, and macro creators (100K+) increasingly price per campaign package rather than per post.

Platform changes the price

A YouTube integration generally costs several times more than a single Instagram Reel from the same creator, reflecting production time and the longer shelf life of the content. Story mentions sit at the lower end, often used as an add-on rather than priced separately.

Niche matters more than most brands expect

Finance and tech creators frequently command higher rates than lifestyle creators at a comparable follower count, since brands in those categories have fewer credible options and higher customer value per conversion.

Tier-1 vs tier-2 and tier-3 cities

Creators based outside metro cities often price lower than their Delhi or Mumbai counterparts at the same follower count, while frequently delivering stronger regional engagement, making them a cost-efficient option for city-specific launches.

What's usually included, and what costs extra

Usage rights beyond the original post, whitelisting for paid ads, and exclusivity periods are rarely included in a base rate and should be negotiated and priced separately up front.

How to budget for a first campaign

Brands running a first campaign generally get better data by spreading budget across several nano or micro creators rather than concentrating it in a single macro partnership.
