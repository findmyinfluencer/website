Building a Media Kit That Actually Gets You Booked

FMI Find My Influencer · June 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Brands reviewing campaign applicants spend, on average, well under a minute deciding whether to open a full profile or scroll past it. Your media kit is what fills that minute, and most first-time creators leave out exactly the details that would make a brand stop scrolling.

What to include

  • A one-line bio: niche, location and what makes your content distinct
  • Audience stats: follower count, engagement rate, and top age/gender/location breakdown
  • Screenshots of platform-native analytics, not just numbers you've typed out
  • 3-5 examples of past brand collaborations, with format and performance if you have it
  • A simple rate card or starting price range per deliverable type
  • Direct contact details, not just a link to your Instagram bio

The five things creators forget

Audience location and demographic breakdown is the single most common omission, and it's often the deciding factor for brands targeting a specific city or age group. Second is engagement rate: brands increasingly filter on this before follower count, so leaving it out makes your profile harder to shortlist. Third is proof, meaning screenshots rather than claimed numbers, since unverifiable stats get discounted by default. Fourth is a rate range, which forces every brand to message you individually to ask, slowing down your own pipeline. Fifth is simply keeping it updated: a media kit from eight months ago undersells a creator who has grown since.

Formatting tips

Keep it to one page if possible, two at most. A brand marketer reviewing 15 applicants for a campaign is not going to read a five-page PDF. Free tools like Canva or Google Slides work fine, exported as a PDF or image, no design software required.

Where your media kit lives on Find My Influencer

Your platform profile already covers most of this automatically (verified stats, niche, location), which is one reason verified creators get more applications reviewed. Keep a standalone media kit as well for pitches you send outside the platform, where brands can't see your verified profile directly.
