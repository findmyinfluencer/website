Micro vs Macro Influencers: Which Delivers Better ROI for Indian Brands?

FMI Find My Influencer · May 27, 2026 · 7 min read

“Should we book one big influencer or several smaller ones?” is one of the most common questions brands bring to their first campaign. The honest answer depends on what you're optimising for, but the underlying data across follower tiers is consistent enough to build a decision framework around.

Defining the tiers

  • Nano: under 10K followers, often the most trusted voice in a very specific community
  • Micro: 10K-100K, the sweet spot for engagement rate relative to reach
  • Mid-tier: 100K-500K, broader reach with engagement typically declining
  • Macro and celebrity: 500K+, maximum reach, lowest engagement rate as a percentage

Cost-per-engagement tends to favour smaller tiers

Engagement rate reliably declines as follower count rises, which means the cost to generate a single like, comment or share is usually lower with micro-influencers than with macro names, even though the macro creator's flat rate is higher. For campaigns optimising for engagement or conversion rather than raw impressions, micro-influencers frequently deliver more for the same budget.

Trust and purchase intent

Audiences consistently rate micro and nano creators as more relatable and trustworthy than macro influencers or celebrities, largely because the relationship feels closer to a recommendation from a peer than an endorsement deal. For considered purchases, where trust matters more than awareness, this tends to translate into higher conversion rates from smaller-tier creators.

Where macro and celebrity still win

Pure awareness and reach are still macro-influencer strengths. If your goal is to get a new product in front of the largest possible audience quickly, a single macro or celebrity post can outperform any number of micro-influencers on raw impressions, even if the engagement rate is lower.

A practical allocation model

Most successful campaigns we track don't pick one tier exclusively. A common pattern is a small number of macro or mid-tier creators for initial reach, layered with a larger bench of micro-influencers for sustained engagement and trust-building over the following weeks. Treat tier selection as a budget allocation decision tied to your specific goal, not a binary choice.
