How Nano-Influencers in India Are Landing Paid Brand Deals

FMI Find My Influencer · May 13, 2026 · 6 min read

It's a common assumption that paid brand deals only start once you cross some follower threshold. In practice, a growing share of campaign budgets on Find My Influencer go to nano-influencers, creators under 10,000 followers, and the reason has less to do with reach and everything to do with what nano creators offer that bigger accounts can't.

Why brands are booking nano creators

A nano creator's audience is usually a genuine community rather than a broad, loosely-connected following, which means recommendations land with a level of trust that's difficult to replicate at scale. Brands running hyperlocal launches, testing a new product category, or working with tighter budgets increasingly treat nano creators as a distinct, valuable tier rather than a stepping stone to “real” influencer status.

Position your account around a specific angle

Generalist nano accounts are harder for brands to place. An account clearly focused on, for example, budget home cooking in a specific city, or skincare for a specific skin concern, gives a brand an obvious reason to book you over a similar-sized generalist account.

Lead with engagement rate, always

This is the single strongest card a nano creator holds. Many nano accounts run engagement rates of 8-15%, far above what most macro accounts achieve. Put this number front and centre in every pitch and profile, since it's the metric most likely to override a brand's default assumption that bigger is better.

What kind of deliverables suit nano creators

  • Product seeding with an honest review post, often the easiest first deal to land
  • Local business collaborations: cafes, salons, gyms and other location-based brands
  • Affiliate or promo-code arrangements, which reward genuine advocacy over reach

Building toward paid, not just gifted, work

Product-for-post arrangements are a reasonable way to build a portfolio early, but treat them as a stepping stone rather than a default. Once you have 3-4 collaborations to show, start quoting a small paid rate rather than continuing to work for product alone.
