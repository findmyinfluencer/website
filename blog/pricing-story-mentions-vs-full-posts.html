How to Price Story Mentions vs Full Posts

FMI Find My Influencer · August 23, 2026 · 5 min read

New creators often default to a single flat rate for anything a brand asks for, whether it's a full feed post or a quick story mention. That undercharges for the bigger deliverable and overcharges for the smaller one, and brands notice both.

Story mentions: fast, lower effort, priced accordingly

A story disappears in 24 hours and typically takes minutes to produce. Pricing it as a smaller add-on, often 15-25% of a feed post rate, reflects both the lower production time and the shorter shelf life.

Feed posts: the anchor deliverable

A feed post lives on a profile indefinitely and usually takes real production time, planning the shot, writing the caption, editing. This is generally where a creator's base rate should be set, with everything else priced relative to it.

Reels and video sit above both

Video content takes meaningfully longer to script, shoot and edit than a photo post, and typically earns more watch time and reach. Pricing it above a static feed post reflects that added production cost.

Bundle discounts should still protect your floor

Offering a modest discount for a multi-deliverable package is reasonable, but each individual piece should still price above your minimum acceptable rate, not be given away to close the bundle.

Put it all in a simple rate card

A one-page rate card listing story, post, Reel and bundle pricing saves back-and-forth negotiation and signals a creator who runs their work like a business.
