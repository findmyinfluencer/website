How a Regional Sweets Brand Used Food Vloggers for a Festive Push

FMI Find My Influencer · August 24, 2026 · 6 min read

A decades-old regional sweets brand had strong local recognition but almost no presence with younger, online-first customers heading into festival season. A food-vlogger campaign was their first attempt at creator marketing, run under a tight three-week deadline.

Finding the right tone

The brand was wary of creator content feeling gimmicky against a traditional product. Working with food vloggers who genuinely covered regional and traditional cuisine, rather than trend-driven food content, kept the campaign feeling authentic.

Twelve creators, one consistent story

Twelve local food vloggers were briefed around a single story: the brand's traditional recipe process, rather than twelve different angles, giving the campaign a coherent identity across very different creator styles.

Timing posts around gifting, not just taste

Content was scheduled ten days before the festival, aligned with when audiences were actively planning gifting purchases rather than just browsing food content.

Measuring beyond likes

In-store and online orders were tracked against a festival-specific promo code shared by every creator, giving the brand a direct read on sales impact rather than relying on view counts.

The results

Festive-season orders rose well above the brand's typical seasonal baseline, with the brand's owners noting it was their first campaign that reached a meaningfully younger customer base.
