How a Travel Brand Partnered With Regional Vloggers to Boost Bookings 2x

FMI Find My Influencer · August 11, 2026 · 6 min read

A domestic travel booking brand had run several campaigns with English-language travel vloggers with modest results, mostly view counts with little booking activity to show for it. The pattern changed once they shifted budget toward regional-language creators.

The problem with the existing approach

English-language travel content performed well in metro cities but converted poorly with the brand's fastest-growing customer segment: tier-2 and tier-3 city travellers planning their first flight booking.

Switching to regional-language vloggers

The brand partnered with 15 Hindi, Marathi and Telugu-language travel vloggers who covered domestic routes and budget travel, a segment underserved by the largest English-language creators in the category.

Content built around real trip planning, not scenery

Instead of destination highlight reels, creators walked through actual booking steps, price comparisons and travel tips in their own language, content that functioned closer to a recommendation than an ad.

Tracking with app referral codes

Each creator's audience used a unique referral code inside the app, letting the brand measure bookings directly rather than relying on view counts as a proxy for success.

The results

Bookings attributed to the campaign roughly doubled compared to the brand's prior English-language creator campaigns, at a lower average cost per booking.
