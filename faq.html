Frequently asked questions

Everything brands and creators ask us most, grouped by topic. Can't find your answer? Reach out on the Contact page.

For Brands

How do I find the right influencer for my brand?
Use the Browse by Niche section to filter creators by category, then compare followers, engagement rate and location on each profile. You can shortlist creators directly and invite them to a campaign.
How much does it cost to launch a campaign?
You set your own budget range when creating a campaign and pay creators directly. Find My Influencer only charges a small campaign management fee, shown upfront before you publish.
Can I run campaigns with multiple creators at once?
Yes. Most brands on the platform run campaigns with anywhere from 5 to 50+ creators at a time. You can review and approve applicants individually or in bulk from your campaign dashboard.

For Influencers

How do I get verified as a creator?
Create your profile, connect your Instagram or YouTube account, and our team reviews your engagement and authenticity within 2 business days. Verified creators get a badge and appear higher in brand search results.
How and when do I get paid?
Payment amount, timeline and method are agreed directly with the brand for each campaign. Find My Influencer doesn't process payments on your behalf, so confirm the details in writing with the brand before you start work.
Is there a minimum follower count to join?
No. We welcome nano and micro-influencers as well as larger creators; many brands specifically look for smaller, highly-engaged audiences for niche campaigns.

General

Is Find My Influencer free to use?
Creating a profile is free for both brands and creators. Brands pay a small platform fee only when they launch a campaign; creators never pay any fee to apply for a campaign.
Which platforms are supported?
Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp and Snapchat creators can all be discovered and booked through the platform, with more platforms being added regularly.
How do I contact support?
Reach our support team any time through the Contact page, or email us directly; we typically respond within one business day.
